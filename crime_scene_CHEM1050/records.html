The Montana–Campbell Family Archive

A documentary history of two established local families, their businesses, property interests, and a long-running dispute over the former Riverside Works tract.

CLASSROOM SIMULATION. Every person, business, address, case number, quotation, and record on this page is fictional and created for this exercise. These are illustrative mock records, not authentic government or newspaper records. The archive contains only information framed as publicly documented; it does not disclose or establish any private relationship between Roberto Montana and Julie Campbell.

How to use this archive

Search names, places, companies, case numbers, or phrases. Filter by record type or family. Select any entry to read its abstract, the relevant public-facing details, and a note about what the record can—and cannot—establish.

Records are arranged by date. Some entries are excerpts or summaries prepared for the simulation; treat them as leads to evaluate, not as proof of every allegation.

Research note: Public records can document that a filing was made, a deed was recorded, a company was registered, or a newspaper reported a claim. They do not automatically prove that an allegation is true, that a person knew about an event, or that a record is connected to the deaths in Case 0247. Distinguish recorded fact, attributed allegation, inference, and unknown information.
